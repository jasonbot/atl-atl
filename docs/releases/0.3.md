# Atl-Atl: The Atltuner 0.3

This release gives Atl-Atl its new screen, built from the design in `assets/ui.svg`. The sound engine is the same as
0.2.

## What is new

- **A new look.** The screen is drawn from a 240 × 240 image: the big note box, BPM area, sample area, four control
  boxes, recording light, loop/once signs, mask sign and latch light.
- **The controls show their values.** Speed, mix, correction and octave are under their labels.
- **The note box** shows the note you are tuning to and the pitch the FM-1 hears.
- **The tempo sign beats.** The BPM label and dot are white when idle, and they switch between green and white on
  each beat while a sample plays or MIDI clock arrives.
- **Sample and tempo text** sit in their boxes with clear labels. The sample shows its length and loop division.
- **The mode row** has once, loop and mask signs, and the latch light is in the top corner.

The package is `atlatl-0.3.fwsc` for the 48 kHz codec. The firmware identity stays `FM-1_922`.
